Assessment of paschata karma (Heading)
VYAPADA AND ITS MANAGEMENT(Sub Heading-1)
Niruha Vyapada and its Management-(cha si 7)
AYOGA
CHIKITSA: ushna pramathya, swedana, phaqlavarti, virechana, bilvamuladi Tikshna basti,
ATIYOGA
CHIKITSA: sheeta basti, piccha basti, anuvasan basti with ghrit manda, prishnaparnadi basti mixed with ghrita
KLAMA(MENTAL FATIGUE)
CHIKITSA: agnideepanartha Kashaya prepared out of pippali etc drugs mixed with lavana should be given. Prasanna, Asava, Arishta proyoga should be done. Gomutra Basti is advised. Swedana and Virukshana measures should be carried out.
ADHMANA(FLATULENCE)
CHIKITSA : Shyamaphaladi siddha taila Anuvasana, Guda varti, Dashamoola and Gomutra siddha Niruha Basti is administered in Admana Vyapat
HIKKA(HICCUP)
CHIKITSA : Bruhmana chikitsa is advised in Hikka. Bala stiradi varga siddha taila anuvasana is advised. Vatanashaka Dhuma, leha, mamsarasa, Ksheera, Sweda are advised
HADPRAPTI
CHIKITSA : amla and lavana skandha siddha niruha basti,vataghna Dravya siddha anuvasana basti
UDAVARTA
CHIKITSA : If patient gets Murcha, then firs Sheeta ambu sechana over the face. Mardana should be carried out over Parshwa and udara.The hairs should be pulled upwards. He should be feared by telling names of Gaja etc animals. Throat should be covered by hand or cloth and slight pressure should be given. By this Apana Vayu will move down normally. After this patient should be given kramuka jala for drinking.If the Basti gets strucked in Pakwashaya, then Bilwadi dwipanchamula Basti should be given. If the medicine reaches Shiras, Navana, Dhuma, lepa of Sarshapa should be applied.
PRAVAHIKA 
CHIKITSA : Abhyanga should be done with Lavana taila. Pradhamana nasya and dhumapana should be carried out to cause shirovirechana. After food Teekshna and Vatanulomaka dravya Saadhita Anuvasana Basti is given.
SHIRO ARTI
CHIKITSA : Abhyanga should be done with Lavana taila. Pradhamana nasya and dhumapana should be carried out to cause shirovirechana. After food Teekshna and Vatanulomaka dravya Saadhita Anuvasana Basti is given.
ANGA ARTI 
CHIKITSA : Abhyanga should be carried out with lavana taila, Ushna jala Sinchana. After this Niruha Basti should be given with Yava, kola kulattha panchamula siddha Kwatha
PARIKARTIKA
CHIKITSA : Basti with Madhura-sheeta dravya, ksheera Basti prepared out of Sarjarasa, Yastimadhu, Jingini, Rasanjana kalka, Madhura-Amla rasa pradhana diet should be given
PARISRAVA 
CHIKITSA : Piccha Basti prepared out of Shalmali Pushpa Vrunta, aja dugdha or Vatankura+aja dugdha. Madhura gana Sadhita Sheetala qwatha sechana over Guda, rakta pitta-atisara nashaka chikitsa should be done.
Anuvasana Vyapada and its Management
VATAVRUTA SNEHA
CHIKITSA : rasnadi Tikshna basti, rasnadi siddha taila anuvasana basti
PITTAVRUTA SNEHA
CHIKITSA : basti with swadu-tiktadravyas
KAPHAVRUTA SNEHA
CHIKITSA : Tikshna basti
ANNA VRUTA SNEHA
CHIKITSA : pachana with katu and lavanachurna and kwath, mrudu virechana, amaharan kriya
PURISHA VRUTA SNEHA 
CHIKITSA : snehana, swedana, varti,. Shyamadibilwadi siddha niruha and anuvasana basti
ABHUKTA PRANITA
CHIKITSA : niruha basti prepared with Kashaya dravyas(Shyama,trivrutta,yava,kola,kulattha,gomutra) and anuvasan basti with the above drugs should be used
Note- In more serious complications, the patient should be transferred to a higher center.
